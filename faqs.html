Frequently Asked questions?

Where can I source rare metals and minerals?

You can source them directly from mining companies, licensed miners, mineral processors, refineries, authorized distributors, and established commodity trading companies.

What documents should accompany a mineral shipment?

Common documents include a commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of analysis, certificate of origin where applicable, transport documents, export permits, and customs declarations. Exact requirements depend on the mineral and the countries involved.

How are rare metals and minerals priced?

Prices may depend on: Metal content, grade, and purity. Global benchmark prices and market demand. Processing and refining costs. Quantity, packaging, and delivery terms. Export restrictions, transport costs, and applicable taxes. Some minerals trade on established exchanges or reference-price markets, while others are priced through private negotiations

How can I confirm the quality and purity of a mineral?

Request a certificate of analysis (COA) and arrange independent testing by a qualified laboratory. Depending on the material, testing may use X-ray fluorescence (XRF), inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry (ICP-MS), or other appropriate analytical methods.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

There is no universal MOQ. Some suppliers sell laboratory samples or small quantities, while mining companies and industrial processors may require orders measured in tonnes.

Can you provide a detailed breakdown of the Mineral process?

Absolutely, we're happy to explain each step of the process to your satisfaction.

What payment methods are commonly used?

Depending on the transaction, parties may use bank transfers, letters of credit, documentary collections, or escrow arrangements where suitable and legally available. Payment milestones should be linked to verified documentation, inspection, and agreed delivery terms.

Are your technicians certified or trained in specific areas?

Yes, our technicians are highly skilled and certified in various areas of Mineral and Metals quality analysis.

Should I pay for a sample before placing a bulk order?

A representative sample can be useful, but establish its origin, sampling method, and testing requirements first. For high-value purchases, arrange independent sampling and agree in writing on the acceptance criteria before committing to a bulk order.

Do I need a license to trade or export rare minerals?

Possibly. Requirements vary by country, mineral type, transaction, and whether you are mining, trading, processing, importing, or exporting. Some minerals are subject to special permits, quotas, or export restrictions.

What is responsible mineral sourcing?

Responsible sourcing means verifying that minerals are obtained and traded in accordance with applicable laws and appropriate environmental, human-rights, labor, and anti-corruption standards.

How can I verify that minerals are conflict-free?

Conduct supply-chain due diligence, identify the mine and intermediaries where possible, review chain-of-custody records, and assess risks of conflict financing, forced labor, corruption, and human-rights abuses. Third-party audits can help but do not replace your own due diligence.

What is traceability in mineral sourcing?

Traceability is the ability to track a mineral's origin and movement through the supply chain, from the mine or source through processing, trading, and delivery.

How are rare minerals shipped internationally?

Depending on the material and its classification, shipments may use air freight, sea freight, or road transport. Packaging, labeling, insurance, and handling must meet applicable transport and customs requirements.

How long does international sourcing take?

Samples may take days or weeks, while commercial orders can take several weeks or months. Lead times depend on availability, testing, export permits, customs clearance, processing, and transport.

What are Incoterms, and why do they matter?

Incoterms clarify key responsibilities between buyers and sellers, including delivery, transport costs, risk transfer, and certain customs obligations. The contract should specify the applicable Incoterm and named place or port.

How do I calculate the landed cost of a mineral?

Include the purchase price, inspection and assay fees, inland transport, export charges, freight, insurance, import duties, taxes, customs clearance, storage, and any refining costs.

Which rare metals and minerals are in high demand?

Materials associated with batteries, electronics, renewable energy, aerospace, and advanced manufacturing include lithium, cobalt, graphite, nickel, copper, tantalum, tungsten, and selected rare earth elements. Demand and prices vary by application and market conditions.

Is sourcing rare minerals profitable?

It can be, but profitability depends on the difference between the verified purchase cost and achievable selling price after processing, logistics, taxes, financing, compliance, and losses. Price volatility, counterparty fraud, and changing regulations can significantly affect returns.

How can I establish long-term supply agreements?

Define material specifications, assay procedures, quantities, pricing formulas, delivery schedules, payment terms, inspection rights, rejection procedures, regulatory obligations, and dispute-resolution mechanisms. Qualify alternative suppliers to reduce dependence on a single source.
